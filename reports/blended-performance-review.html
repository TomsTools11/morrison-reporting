Recommendations

1
Staff the 7–9 AM window
537 of 707 leads (75.95%) arrive between 7 AM and 9 AM. Contact rate in the 7 AM hour is 36.86% against 58.33% at 9 AM; quote rate is 16.62% against 33.33%.
2
Respond within 5 minutes
284 of 707 leads have been reached (40.17%). Contact rates fall sharply after the first few minutes; new leads should be the highest-priority task in the queue, and a text after a missed call raises callback rates.

Keep disposition reporting current
Quote and bind outcomes come from the CRM export. Continuous reporting keeps quote rate, bind counts and CPA accurate at the source and county level, and directs budget toward the segments that close.
