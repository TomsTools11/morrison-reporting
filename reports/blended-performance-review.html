07

Recommendations

1
Hold budget concentration on proven sources
On Auto, Premium Referral produced 5 of the 7 bound policies and the search, email, and SEO sources are quoting at $65.09 per quote. On Home, Tier 1 produced the most leads, the most quotes, and the bound policy. We are keeping the budget weighted to these sources.
2
Resolve the two open delivery reviews with the platform team
Auto Native/Display/Social holds the largest pool of available auctions and is winning no ad placements; Home Tier 1 wins 1% of the auctions it enters. Both reviews are continuing.
3
Close the contact-rate gaps on Premium Referral and Tier 2
101 of 260 Premium Referral leads (38.8%) and 5 of 26 Tier 2 leads (19.2%) were contacted, against 45.3% across the account. Routing these leads into the standard follow-up cadence closes the gap.
4
Work every lead on the 30-day follow-up cadence
Our recommended 30-day, 60+ touch cadence across phone, text, and email: GOAL 30-Day Lead Follow-Up Playbook →
