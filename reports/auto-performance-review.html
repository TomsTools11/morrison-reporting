06

Recommendations

1
Concentrate budget on the proven sources
Premium Referral produced 102 of 154 leads, 7 quotes, and the top win rate at 20.4%. Email, Internal SEO 2, and Premium Paid Search 3 each convert leads to quotes efficiently. We are holding Premium Referral's placement and directing spend to these sources.
2
Resolve Native/Display/Social delivery
This source holds the largest pool of available auctions and is winning no ad placements at its current bid. We are continuing to work it with the platform team to restore delivery or reallocate that demand to converting sources.
3
Weight bids toward multi-vehicle households
Two-or-more-vehicle households are 35% of Auto leads, up from 29% last period. We are keeping the multi-vehicle modifier raised to grow that share, the multi-car segment the agency prioritizes.
4
Work every lead on the 30-day follow-up cadence
Contact rate was 34.4% and 34.0% of contacted leads were quoted. Consistent follow-up across phone, text, and email is what moves leads to quotes and binds: GOAL 30-Day Lead Follow-Up Playbook →
