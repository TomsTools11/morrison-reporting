Recommendations

1
Hold the concentration in Harris and the DFW counties
Harris, Dallas, Tarrant, and Fort Bend account for 297 of 570 leads and 68 of 113 quotes, at $83.84–$99.12 per quote. Dallas and Tarrant carry the highest quote rates of the major counties (25.0% and 24.4%). County is the most granular geographic lever on the platform; current delivery is already weighted to these markets.
2
Close the follow-up gap in Bexar before any targeting change
Bexar delivered 37 leads with a 40.5% contact rate and a 10.8% quote rate ($194.66 per quote), against 45.3% and 19.8% account-wide. We are routing Bexar leads through the standard follow-up cadence and re-reading the county after the gap closes; no geographic exclusion is recommended.
3
Track the high-conversion satellite cities
Arlington (83.3% contact, 2 policies), Humble (81.2% contact), Katy (41.7% quote rate), Irving (40.0%), and Tyler (36.4%, 1 policy) convert above the account average on 10–16 leads each. These sit inside currently targeted counties; we are monitoring them as the volume grows.
4
Re-run this view against the Allstate target-county list
The May 2026 ZIP overlap analysis pre-dates this data. Stacking the current county performance against the newer Allstate target-county list aligns carrier priorities with the counties that are producing quotes and policies.
