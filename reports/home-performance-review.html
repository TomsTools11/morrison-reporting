06

Recommendations

1
Prioritize single-family, new-customer households
On June 18 we added an existing-Allstate-customer exclusion and raised the Single Family Home modifier while reducing bids on renter property types (apartment, condominium, duplex, townhome). Single-family homes are 98% of leads and all 9 quotes this period.
2
Improve top-of-wall visibility on Tier 1
Tier 1 holds the widest search coverage and produced the most Home leads (26). We are reviewing its ad-wall placement to win more of that available volume.
3
Hold the converting tiers
Tier 3 produced the most quotes (4 of 9) and Tier 2 added steady volume at a 12.1% win rate. Placement is steady; we are leaving these unchanged.
4
Maintain the reduced bid on Home Search
Market clearing prices for this source run well above the rest of the campaign. Budget is better deployed across Tier 1 through Tier 3.
5
Work every lead on the 30-day follow-up cadence
Contact rate was 32.7% and half of contacted leads were quoted. Consistent follow-up across phone, text, and email is what moves leads to quotes and binds: GOAL 30-Day Lead Follow-Up Playbook →
