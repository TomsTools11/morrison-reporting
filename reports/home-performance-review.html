Recommendations

1
Resolve Tier 1 auction delivery
Tier 1 holds the largest pool of available auctions and produced the most leads, the most quotes, and the bound policy, but wins 1% of the auctions it enters. The review with the platform team is continuing.
2
Hold the quality-focused targeting
The existing-Allstate-customer exclusion and the single-family property focus added June 18 remain active. 47.5% of leads were contacted and 39.0% of contacted leads were quoted under these settings.
3
Prioritize outreach on Tier 2 leads
5 of 26 Tier 2 leads were contacted (19.2%), against 56.4% on Tier 1 and 46.4% on Tier 3. Routing these leads into the standard follow-up cadence closes the gap.
4
Work every lead on the 30-day follow-up cadence
Our recommended 30-day, 60+ touch cadence across phone, text, and email: GOAL 30-Day Lead Follow-Up Playbook →
